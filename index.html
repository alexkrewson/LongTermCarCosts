Car Cost Graph

Compare up to three ways of owning a car over the long run. Today's dollars.

General

How it's modeled: a swap car loses value smoothly (same % each year), so it's worth the resale % after the years kept; each swap buys an identical car at the same price and pays the transaction fee. A keep-forever car is bought once and assumed worth $0 at the end. Maintenance follows the age brackets as the car ages; the oldest bracket is the steady-state rate. Big repairs (engine/transmission, or battery for an EV) recur every so many years of vehicle age; a car bought already past that age gets its first one half an interval in. With full wear on, swap cars pay the steady-state rate and big repairs averaged per year, so wear isn't passed on to the next owner; keep-forever cars always pay their own repairs as they happen. DIY maintenance is 60% of the shop brackets. Insurance, registration and inflation are left out.
