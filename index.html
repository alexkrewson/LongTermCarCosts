How it's modeled: the sawtooth car loses value smoothly (same % each year), so it's worth the resale % after the years kept. Each swap buys an identical car at the same price and pays the transaction fee (tax, title, dealer fees). Its maintenance follows the age brackets as it ages. The old car's maintenance is a flat yearly average. Its first big spike comes half an interval in, since the car is already old, and then repeats every interval. The timing belt is mileage-based. The old car is assumed to be worth $0 at the end. Insurance, registration and inflation are left out.
